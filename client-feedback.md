Client View – Client Journey Needs Review

I’m reviewing the Client login and the current flow feels incomplete. The client dashboard should focus first on helping clients find a student/service and arrange a practice service, rather than leading with services already received.

Please review the client experience with this structure:

1. Find a Service / Student — primary action

Search or browse available services.
Search/browse students offering those services.
Filter by service, location, availability, institution, etc.
View a student profile.
View the student’s verified portfolio before requesting a service.
Request/book a service with the student. 2. Upcoming Services

Show requested and confirmed appointments.
Allow the client to view service details.
Message the student.
Accept/decline appointment requests or suggest another time.
Reschedule/cancel where appropriate. 3. Confirm Service Completion

After the student completes the service, the client should receive a clear request to confirm that the service took place.

I currently see Confirm and Dispute. I would change Dispute to something softer and clearer such as:

Confirm Service | Report an Issue

If the client selects Report an Issue, they should choose a reason such as:

Service was cancelled
Service was incomplete
I did not receive this service
Service details are incorrect
Other
This should flag the record for review rather than automatically treating the student submission as invalid.

4. Service History

Services already received can remain under History, rather than occupying the main client dashboard.

Expected client journey:

Find Service → Find Student → View Portfolio → Check Availability → Request Appointment → Confirm Appointment → Receive Service → Confirm Completion → Service Moves to History

I also need the Confirm Service process completed so I can test what happens after the client accepts the service. At present I cannot see the full journey through to the student record and educator verification stage.
